# Original synthetic mechanics pilot

These five practice prompts were authored for SaemPhysics as synthetic test
data. They are not copied from an exam, textbook, or uploaded reference.
The set exercises the project's existing one-dimensional
constant-acceleration solver with positive, zero, and negative acceleration.

1. A cart moves at 3 m/s and accelerates at 2 m/s^2 for 4 s. Find its
   displacement and final velocity.
2. A runner moves at 4 m/s and accelerates at 1.5 m/s^2 for 2 s. Find the
   displacement and final velocity.
3. A vehicle moves at 5 m/s and accelerates at -1 m/s^2 for 3 s. Find the
   displacement and final velocity.
4. A cart moves at -2 m/s and accelerates at 2 m/s^2 for 2 s. Find the
   displacement and final velocity.
5. A cyclist moves at 3 m/s with zero acceleration for 5 s. Find the
   displacement and final velocity.

This source file contains question statements only. It is not an official
answer key, and the associated canonical records remain unsolved and
unreviewed until a reviewer deliberately updates them.
