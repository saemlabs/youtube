THE IDEA

Learning should be clear, checkable, and honest.

SaemPhysics is being developed to help students practice physics and mathematics, see how a result was obtained, and distinguish checked calculations from teaching explanations that still need a teacher's review.

WHAT IS IN DEVELOPMENT
